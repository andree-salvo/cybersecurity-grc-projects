# Project 3: Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Objective

The goal of this project is to conduct a cybersecurity risk assessment for MedBridge Health, Inc., a fictional healthcare company based in San Francisco, California.

I'll use the eight security weaknesses identified in the Company Profile to evaluate potential cybersecurity risks, determine their likelihood and impact, assign risk owners, and recommend appropriate risk treatments.

## Project Scope

This assessment focuses on risks involving asset management, employee security awareness, backup recovery, incident response, vendor compliance, privileged access, security monitoring, and employee offboarding.

## Methodology

I'll use a qualitative risk assessment approach with a 5 × 5 risk scoring matrix.

Each risk will be evaluated based on its likelihood of occurring and its potential impact on MedBridge Health's operations, patient information, and compliance responsibilities.

The final risk scores will help determine which security weaknesses should be addressed first.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets
- GitHub
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

## Deliverables

- Risk Assessment Methodology
- Cybersecurity Risk Register
- Risk Heat Map
- Risk Treatment Plan
- Risk Assessment Report
